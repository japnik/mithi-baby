From Our Family to Yours

Mithi Baby wasn’t built in a boardroom—it was built in a nursery. As a mother to 9-month-old Liv, I wanted my daughter to grow up surrounded by the same sweet Punjabi and Hindi melodies that my own mother sang to me. Living in the U.S., I realized how hard it is to find high-quality, soulful music that feels personal and honors our roots.

I want to create a digital "Village" where our children’s names and our traditions live forever. This YouTube channel is a community library of those moments. When you use the Mithi Baby app to create a song for your little one, you have the option to share it on YouTube, building a permanent digital Virasat (heritage) for your family and ours.

— Navneet (Liv’s Mom), Founder of Mithi Baby
