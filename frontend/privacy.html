Google User Data

Our Service allows you to upload generated videos directly to your own YouTube channel. To facilitate this, we access the following Google User Data:

  • YouTube Data API: We use this API to upload videos you create to your verified YouTube channel.

How we use it: The data (your authentication token) is used strictly to perform the upload action initiated by you. We do not view, delete, or modify other content on your channel.

Storage: Your authentication tokens are stored securely and used only for the purpose of uploading the content you generated.

Sharing: We do not share your Google User Data with any third parties, AI models, or external servers other than Google's own services required to complete the upload.
